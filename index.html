PROJECT_01 // Distributed systems

Consistent Distributed Cache System

Jul to Sep 2026 · ProjectX mentorship

A cache that keeps serving reads after losing any 2 of its 6 nodes.

  • Fault-tolerant cache in Go using Raft consensus, with BoltDB and Pebble persistence and gRPC between nodes.
  • Reed-Solomon erasure coding (4+2 shards), an orchestrator with automated failover, and a 7-node Raft metadata cluster across 15 Docker containers.
  • Stampede-resistant reads rebuilt from the first 4 shard responses, plus a disk-backed 2Q cache with Prometheus metrics and a live dashboard.

Kavach

Oct 2026 to now · Team of 4

Replaces risky dependencies, then checks the AI did not cheat.
